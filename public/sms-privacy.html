Ref OS SMS Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026

Information We Collect

Ref OS collects mobile phone numbers only from designated event administrators who voluntarily agree to receive operational text message alerts.

How Mobile Information Is Used

Mobile phone numbers are used only to send time sensitive Ref OS operational alerts. These alerts may include volunteer help requests and requests to regenerate volunteer access codes.

Mobile Information Sharing

Mobile information, text messaging consent, and opt in information will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Message Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies based on event activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting Out

Recipients may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving text messages. Reply HELP for assistance.
