Ref OS SMS Terms and Conditions

Effective September 21, 2026

Program Description

Ref OS sends operational text message alerts to designated event administrators. Messages may include volunteer help requests and requests to regenerate volunteer role access codes.

Consent

Messages are sent only to administrators who voluntarily provide their mobile number and agree to receive Ref OS operational alerts. Consent to receive messages is not a condition of purchase.

Message Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies based on event activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting Out

Reply STOP to cancel text messages. After opting out, the recipient will no longer receive Ref OS SMS alerts unless they provide consent again.
